# Project brief

## Project Title

# GIS-Based Mapping and Accessibility Analysis of Schools in North-Eastern Nigeria

# Background of study

This project uses GIS to examine the distribution and accessibility of schools in North-Eastern Nigeria. School location data obtained from HDX will be analysed using QGIS.

# Aim
To map schools and assess their spatial accessibility to surrounding communities.
 
# Objective 

# The specific objectives are to:

1.Obtain and prepare school location data from HDX.
2.Map the spatial distribution of schools in North-Eastern Nigeria.
3.Examine the distribution of schools across the states or administrative areas in the study area.
4.Analyse accessibility to schools using distance and/or proximity-based GIS techniques.
5.Identify areas that are relatively well served and areas with limited access to schools.
6.Produce maps and spatial outputs that can support educational infrastructure planning.

# Research Questions

1.Where are schools located in North-Eastern Nigeria?
2.How are schools spatially distributed across the study area?
3.Which areas have a high concentration of schools?
4.Which areas have relatively few schools?
5.How close are settlements to schools?
6.Which areas are within reasonable distances of schools?
7.Which areas may have limited accessibility to schools?
8.How can GIS support the planning and management of educational facilities?

# Methodology
1.Identifying areas with limited spatial access.
2.Checking the coordinate reference system (CRS).
3.Importing the data into QGIS
4.Downloadng school data from HDX.
